Color Buddy is a tool to visualize how colors appear to people with different types of colorblindness.
The three types of colorblindness we'll be focusing on will be protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia.

Protanopia: A less common type of red-green colorblindness where shades of red look more green and less bright.

Deuteranopia: The most common type of red-green color blindness, where it makes certain shades of green look more red.

Tritanopia: The least common type of color-blindness where someone is unable to tell the difference between blue and green, purple and red, and yellow and pink. It also makes colors look less bright.

It is estimated that 8% of men, and 0.5% of women are colorblind totaling about 300 million people worldwide.
With this many people having some type of colorblindness it should be common practice for websites, apps, and other programs to be designed inclusively, ensuring that colorblind users can perceive content clearly.

Enter an RGB triple (0–255) and click Simulate.
You'll get the original color plus protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia simulations.
